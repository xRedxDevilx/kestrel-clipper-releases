# Kestrel Clipper

**Your clipper never sleeps.** It watches the streams you care about around the clock, spots the moments worth posting, and hands you a finished vertical short with captions, ready for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

Everything runs on your own PC. Your footage is never uploaded to anyone's servers.

### [⬇ Download for Windows](https://github.com/xRedxDevilx/kestrel-clipper-releases/releases/latest)

New here? Follow the **[setup guide](docs/SETUP.md)** — it walks through the install, including the blue
Windows warning you will see, and getting your first clip.

Want to understand what everything does? The **[full manual](docs/MANUAL.md)** covers every page and setting,
what the AI costs to run, and how to get better clips out of it.

## What it does

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Watches live** | Add a Twitch, YouTube or Kick channel. When they go live it starts recording, 24/7, whether or not you are at the PC. |
| **Finds the moment** | It reads chat and listens to the stream. When chat explodes or the streamer reacts, that moment is cut out and scored. |
| **Edits it for you** | Vertical 9:16, facecam kept on screen, karaoke captions, swearing bleeped, the hook on screen in the first seconds. |
| **Writes the post** | Title, caption and hashtags for each clip, ready to copy. |
| **Tracks the money** | Paid clipping campaigns, your posts, the submit deadline on each one, views and what you earned. |
| **Old streams too** | Paste any VOD and it scans the whole thing and ranks the best moments against each other. |

## What you need

- **Windows 10 or 11** (64-bit)
- **About 10 GB free** for recordings and clips
- **The PC awake** while a stream you follow is live — it cannot record a stream while the machine is asleep
- **Optional:** an NVIDIA graphics card makes the editing much faster
- **Optional:** an AI key (Claude, OpenAI or Gemini) for the smartest clip picking. Without one it still works, using chat reaction and audio.

## Getting started

1. [Download the installer](https://github.com/xRedxDevilx/kestrel-clipper-releases/releases/latest)
2. Run it. Windows will show a blue warning the first time — the [setup guide](docs/SETUP.md) shows exactly what to click.
3. Add a streamer, press **Start clipping**, and leave it running.

Clips appear under **Review**. Approve the ones you like, and **Post & submit** gives you the file and the copy.

## Updates

The app checks for updates once a day and tells you when one is out. **Download update** fetches it, checks it is
genuine, and **Install and restart** does the rest. Your clips, streamers and settings are kept.
